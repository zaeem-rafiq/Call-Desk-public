# Delta Air Lines FY2026 guidance as of Q2 2026 (July 10, 2026)

## Headline

The Q2 2026 release headline bullet reads: "Affirming full-year guidance for adjusted EPS of $6.50 to $7.50 and free cash flow of $3 to $4 billion".

## Exact guidance text (Q2 2026 release, page 2, "Financial Guidance")

| | FY 2026 |
|---|---|
| Earnings Per Share | $6.50 - $7.50 |
| Free Cash Flow ($B) | $3 - $4 |
| Gross Leverage (adjusted debt to EBITDAR) | Approx. 2x |

| | 3Q26 |
|---|---|
| Total Revenue YoY (%) | Up Mid-Teens |
| Operating Margin | 11% - 13% |
| Earnings Per Share | $2.00 - $2.50 |

> Guidance for the September quarter assumes fuel at the forward curve as of July 2, 2026, and includes a refinery benefit of 5-cents per gallon. This results in a projected all-in fuel price for the quarter of approximately $3.15 per gallon.

Ed Bastian (CEO), page 1:

> "Delta is executing from a position of strength, and we expect momentum to carry into the second half with double-digit margins and a return to earnings growth. For the full year, we are affirming the guidance we set at the start of the year to grow earnings by 20 percent, overcoming a multi-billion dollar fuel headwind."

Joe Esposito (CCO), page 2:

> "For the September quarter, we expect revenue to grow mid-teens over prior year on modest capacity growth, with unit revenue growth improving sequentially. While still early, current trends provide a constructive setup for this strength to extend into the December quarter."

Erik Snell (CFO), page 3:

> "In the September quarter, we expect earnings per share to grow over prior year to $2.00 to $2.50 on an operating margin of 11 to 13 percent."
>
> "Non-fuel unit cost performance is expected to improve modestly from the June quarter with further progression in the December quarter as capacity growth begins to normalize. This puts us back on a path toward our long-term framework of low-single-digit non-fuel unit cost growth."
>
> "Debt reduction remains a top priority, and we expect to reach gross leverage of approximately 2x by year-end."

## Statements on the Q2 2026 call (July 10, 2026)

These are short statements from the call, not a transcript.

- Erik Snell (CFO) gave the Q3 outlook on the call: an all-in fuel price of about $3.15 per gallon including a 5-cent refinery benefit, total fuel expense about 40% higher than last year, an operating margin of 11% to 13% and EPS of $2.00 to $2.50.
- Ed Bastian (CEO) repeated the full-year EPS outlook of $6.50 to $7.50 (20% growth) and the free cash flow outlook of $3 to $4 billion.
- Joe Esposito (CCO) said Q3 capacity was up 1% and Q4 capacity was planned at 2% to 3%, led by International. He said December quarter bookings were coming in strong.
